Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Fastli is a wellness app for intermittent fasting, nutrition logging, cold exposure, breathing and mindfulness practices. It is made by NutVok Apps and runs as a Telegram Mini App and a Telegram bot, with Android and iOS apps in development. This policy explains what data Fastli collects, why, and what control you have over it.

Data we collect

  • Account: your Telegram user ID, name and language. If you sign in with Google or Apple, the account identifier and email address that provider shares with us.
  • Profile you enter: weight, height, age, sex, activity level, goals, time zone.
  • Your logs: fasts, meals and water, practices (cold, breathing, movement, mindfulness), mood check-ins, weight and body measurements, morning heart rate variability (HRV) and resting heart rate.
  • Data from connected services (only if you connect them): from Garmin Connect, Android Health Connect or Apple Health — HRV, resting heart rate, sleep, steps, weight and activities.
  • Messages to the AI coach and photos of food you choose to send.
  • Technical data: error logs needed to fix problems.

How we use it

  • To show you your own history, trends and progress, and to calculate goals such as calories and water.
  • To answer your messages to the AI coach and recognise food in text or photos.
  • To send reminders you can control with the /notify command in the bot.
  • Health data from connected services is used only to display your own trends to you (for example, HRV against your personal baseline). Fastli does not diagnose or make medical claims.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers or use it for advertising.

Service providers

Fastli uses these processors to run the service:

  • Supabase — database and sign-in.
  • Vercel and Railway — hosting of the app and bot.
  • Anthropic — AI processing of coach messages and food recognition. Only the content needed to answer is sent.
  • USDA FoodData Central — food nutrition lookups (only the food name is sent).
  • Telegram — messaging and payments with Telegram Stars; Tribute — card payments.

Security

Data is stored in a private database with row-level security: each user can access only their own records. Health data tables are restricted to their owner. Secret keys are kept on the server only.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account and all associated data in the app (Profile → Delete account), disconnect any connected service at any time, or ask us to delete your data by writing to nutvok.apps@gmail.com or @fastli_support in Telegram. Disconnecting Garmin or another service stops new data from being received; deleting the account removes the data already stored.

Children

Fastli is not intended for people under 18. Fasting is not suitable for everyone; please talk to a doctor if you have a medical condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or bot.

Contact

NutVok Apps · Email: nutvok.apps@gmail.com · Telegram: @fastli_support

Політика конфіденційності

Оновлено: 14 листопада 2026

Fastli — застосунок для інтервального голодування, обліку їжі, холоду, дихальних практик і медитації від NutVok Apps. Працює як міні-застосунок і бот у Telegram; застосунки для Android та iOS у розробці.

Щоб показувати тобі твою історію, тренди й прогрес, рахувати цілі, відповідати на повідомлення коучу і надсилати нагадування (налаштовуються командою /notify). Дані з годинників використовуються лише для показу твоїх власних трендів. Fastli не ставить діагнозів.
