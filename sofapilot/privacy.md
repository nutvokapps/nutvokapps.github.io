# SofaPilot – Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 30 September 2026

SofaPilot ("the app") is a TV remote control app developed by NutvokApps (Oleksandr Kovtun). This policy explains what data the app uses and how.

## 1. Data that stays on your phone
To find and control your TV, the app works over your local Wi-Fi network. It stores on your phone only: the TV's name, model, local IP address, MAC address (used to turn the TV on over Wi-Fi) and pairing keys issued by the TV. This data is kept in the app's private storage and is never sent to us.

## 2. Permissions
- **Nearby Wi-Fi devices / local network** — to discover TVs on your home network.
- **Microphone** (optional, voice search) — your speech is converted to text by your phone's speech recognition service, and the text is sent to your TV. We do not record or store audio.
- **Notifications and foreground service** — for the sleep timer and remote controls in the notification area.

## 3. Diagnostic log
The app keeps a technical log on your phone (connection steps, phone model, Android version, local network addresses). It is sent to us only if you tap "Send log" and choose to share it. We use it only to fix problems and delete it when it is no longer needed.

## 4. Analytics and crash reports
We use Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes. They collect anonymous usage events (for example "TV connected", "screen opened"), crash reports, device model, OS version and an app-instance identifier. We do not send your name, email, TV addresses, MAC addresses or typed text to these services. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 5. Purchases
Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment card details. To manage subscriptions we may use RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous purchase identifier and purchase status: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## 6. What we do not do
The app does not show ads, does not sell your data, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

## 7. Children
The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 8. Data retention and deletion
Data stored on your phone is deleted when you remove a TV in the app, clear the app's data, or uninstall it. Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. To request deletion of any data you sent us, email nutvok.apps@gmail.com.

## 9. Changes
We may update this policy. The latest version is always available at this address.
